Cursed Pokémon

When night falls in the overworld, Ghost, Dark, and Poison-type Pokémon can spawn Cursed. You don't have to look for them. A Cursed Pokémon will come after you and start a battle, so keep your team ready.

The curse lasts from nightfall until dawn. The rarer the Cursed Pokémon, the more Candy it drops.

Mix it up

Beating the same Pokémon over and over won't work. After 5 wins against the same Pokémon, it stops dropping Candy until you catch a different Pokémon.

10–50 Candy each

Haunted Pumpkaboo

Haunted Pumpkaboo hide all over the overworld every night. They're the biggest Candy prize you can find on your own, so keep your eyes open while you explore.

Once one is beaten or caught, it disappears and shows up somewhere else later.

How an encounter works

  1. Get close to it
  2. It reveals itself
  3. A battle starts
  4. Defeat it or catch it
  5. Collect your Candy

Moon Altar Rituals

Team Rocket's moon altar at spawn charges up several times a day. When a ritual starts, the whole server gets one shared goal and a time limit. Hit the goal to stop the ritual.

Win, and the whole server gets a Lunar Surge buff. Everyone who helped also earns Candy.

Rituals: 60 min Weekend Grand Rituals: 90 min
